## Deadlines

- Submission opens Sat Nov 14, 09:00 and closes **Sun Nov 15, 23:59**, local time of the Local Event.
- "Check with your Local Lead about times for your Local Event's local judging and awards which may differ from the
  global submission times." Our local plan (submit by 15:30, judging 16:00, see `AGENTS.md`) is stricter, so it stands.
- "Any projects saved or submitted past this deadline will not be eligible for judging." Only the last saved submission
  is judged; it can be edited and resubmitted until the deadline.

## The demo (hard limits)

- **Either a video of 30 seconds or less, or a slide deck of at most 7 slides including title and intro slides.**
- "Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging."
- Hosted outside the Space Apps site (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, ...) with a public link: no login, permission or
  registration. Test it in a private browser window.
- English only. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slides must be in English.
- Suggested 30-second video structure:
  - 0:00 names and team name
  - 0:02 which challenge and why
  - 0:05 project title and tagline
  - 0:10 how it works, the user's experience, how NASA open data made it possible
  - 0:20 to 0:30 the future: what it could do for people, the world and beyond
- A slide deck must show: an image of the project, the user experience, how NASA and partner data make it possible,
  the team name, the names of team members, and the challenge selected.
- "the majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission", but a strong demo helps the
  Presentation score.

## Project page fields (all required fields must be filled to submit)

1. **Project Name**
2. **High-Level Project Summary:** what we developed, how it addresses the challenge, why it is important.
3. **Link to Project Demo** (the video or slides above)
4. **Link to Final Project:** public link, no password. For us: the Vercel URL and the GitHub repo.
5. **Detailed Project Description:** what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools, languages, hardware, software.
6. **Use of Super Intelligence (SI)** (see below). "for data-collection purposes only, and your response will not be
   seen by the judges."
7. **NASA Data:** "To be eligible for a Global Award, you must use at least one NASA data or resource."
8. **Space Agency Partner Data and Other References:** "List all of the data, resources, and tools used in your
   project", including code, text and images, even open source ones. Partner agencies are encouraged (ESA, JAXA,
   CSA and others; JAXA, ESA, CNES and DLR are partners in FLARE).

Images on the page: JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB. No video or document upload.

## SI (AI) rules

- AI tools are allowed "as a tool to accelerate innovation and creativity".
- **"SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)."**
- Generated content may not use, contain or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers).
- **Images and videos: must include a visible watermark** indicating AI generation.
- Audio: acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata.
- **Code and data: must acknowledge AI generation in descriptive text and metadata.**
- Judges see these markers and "evaluate originality, intent, and execution based on the SI-assisted elements".

## Other rules

- All team members must be confirmed participants at the same Local Event.
- All content in English.
- No images, video, audio or likeness of anyone under 18.
- At submission: tick "Terms and Conditions" and "Confirm All Work is Original", then "Submit for Judging". A banner
  confirms success.
- Own website: optionally request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification so judges' networks do not block it
  (category such as "web-based application", Company Name "N/A"). Not required.
- Participant certificates appear on each member's profile after submission. The name comes from "Full Name" in the
  profile.

## Judging criteria

Five criteria: **Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation**, each scored 1 to 5, no weights. Details and
the exact wording are in `judging.md`.
