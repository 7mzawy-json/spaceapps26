THE CHALLENGE

What NASA asked for, and where to find it.

Written for the readers the challenge names: professors, students, aerospace researchers and engineers.

"Identify investigations most relevant to the fire safety needs of habitable crew environments"

Pick any oxygen and pressure. Analysis ranks the nine investigations by how many of their tests ran near it, and lists the ones that cannot be placed, with the reason.

"Extract data that supports predictive modeling of flammability and fire scenarios"

Research tools filters and graphs every test by fuel, oxygen, pressure, the other gases in the chamber, burn time and outcome. The CSV download keeps each row's NASA file and line.

"Highlight research that informs the development of next-generation fire safety controls and equipment"

The evidence brief for a point says what is untested there and names the NASA experiments planned to close the gap, such as FM2 on the Moon.

ASK FLASHPOINT

Ask about the test records.

For example, ask which tests used 30% oxygen at 10.2 psi, or what the records say about solid materials. Answers include their sources and identify missing data.

Answers come from the Flashpoint SI assistant (AI) and cite NASA records. If the AI is unavailable, a rule-based fallback answers instead and says so.
