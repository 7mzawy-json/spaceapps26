# Judging and Awards Guide (read 2026-11-14)

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/ (dated Nov 13, 2026).
Summarized in our own words; open the page for the exact text.

## How scoring works

- Every level of judging (local, global, executive) scores each project **1 to 5 on five criteria**:
  Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. The guide gives no weights, so treat them as equal.
- Judges score the demo **and** the full project. The guide warns that teams tend to neglect Presentation and asks
  for equal effort on the pitch and the story.

## The five criteria, and what each means for Flashpoint

| Criterion | What judges look for | How Flashpoint answers it |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | Size of the problem and how many people the solution helps. A 5 could change the world. | Moon Base and rover atmospheres are being chosen now; the map shows designers where fire evidence is missing. Earth spinoffs (detectors, firefighter gear) widen the audience. |
| Creativity | A new concept, or a real improvement on existing ideas. A 5 has never been tried. | Other teams summarize what NASA knows. We map where NASA's knowledge ends, on the chart atmosphere designers already use. |
| Validity | Grounded in science, technically feasible, works for its purpose, usable and user-friendly. A 5 is fully developed and ready for real use. | Every number traces to a PSI accession, missing values stay `null`, the predictor is labelled illustrative, the 3.07 psi curve passes through ISS and Moon Base. The site must be easy to use on a first visit. |
| Relevance | Answers the challenge directly and uses NASA data as a core part, not decoration. | The whole map is built from PSI test rows; the brief cites accessions. Name the PSI investigations in the demo. |
| Presentation | Clear story: the challenge, the solution, why it matters. Engaging and well structured. | The 30-second video or 7 slides (see `submission-and-awards.md`), the scroll story, and the local pitch. |

## Eligibility for Global Judging (all must hold)

- Answer one official challenge.
- Every member registered for the **same Local Event**, not waitlisted, and listed on the team's Members tab.
  Members cannot be added after submission closes. Teams of 1 to 6 at a Local Event.
- Every required field on the project page filled in.
- Demo: at most 7 slides (title and intro included) or a video of at most 30 seconds.
- English on the page and in linked material (a video may be spoken in another language with English subtitles).
- Submitted from the team page before **Sun Nov 15, 23:59 local time** of the Local Event.
- Both checkboxes ticked: the submission-requirements and terms box, and the original-work box. The second box says
  every external resource (code, text, images, even open source) is listed in the References field and that no
  copyrighted material was used without permission.
- NASA reviews teams for prohibited bilateral activity with China or Chinese-owned entities.

## What this changes for us

- The References field is part of a checkbox we sign. Keep a running list of every library, image and dataset from
  the first hour (the AI usage log has a section for it), so nothing is missed on Sunday.
- Validity rewards usability, not only correctness. The Story lane's two-hourly "first-time visitor" click-through
  matters for the score.
- Presentation is scored on the demo and the whole project, so the About page and the site's own text count too.
