Experiment

Flame lab: one NASA test, then your own experiment

Pick a microgravity fire test. Its oxygen, pressure and fuel set up the flame. Then change gravity, add cabin airflow, blow on the flame, or look inside it at the oxygen, the heat and the flow.

The simulation runs in your browser on the graphics card (WebGL 2). If it cannot, a simpler backup flame takes its place. Open the flame lab on its own page.
